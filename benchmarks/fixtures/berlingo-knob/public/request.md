# berlingo-knob — the request

Design a replacement gear-shift knob for a 2014 Citroën Berlingo (B9), from
scratch. There is no source CAD; the lever was measured with calipers and
photographed.

The lever, from the boot surface upward: a base plate that is the natural seat,
a smooth shaft, two opposed rails, a clip button on a face roughly 90° from the
rails, then a taper to the tip. The knob has to slide the full engagement length
without any radial obstruction jamming it, and seat on the plate.

Reverse is push-in; no lift collar is required.

## What this fixture is worth as evidence

`evidence_class: DESIGN_STAGE_UNVERIFIED`. Nothing has been printed. Two of the
driving numbers — the base-plate height and the button height — are photo
estimates the design's own notes give as ±2 mm, and the button protrusion was
never measured at all. Anything this fixture measures is about modelling and
review, not about fit.
