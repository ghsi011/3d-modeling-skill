# vent-ball-combine — the request

Combine two models I have already printed and use:

* a 17 mm male ball joint that fits my MagSafe cradle;
* a Tesla Model 3 air-vent phone mount that grips the vent well.

The vent mount is to carry the ball joint instead of its phone-holding
mechanism. The ball sits 90° to the face the phone rested against. The lips that
hold the phone from above and below come off.

Both source files are recorded in the fixture manifest by path and hash. Nothing
else about the finished part is given here.

## Consequence

Vehicle-mounted, carrying a phone on a magnetic cradle in a moving car. Two
failure modes matter: the ball root snapping, and the vent grip letting go.
Either drops a phone into the footwell of a car in motion.

A reference solid. It is not named here, it is not reachable from anything a
design agent is handed, and its dimensions are not stated anywhere in this
directory.
