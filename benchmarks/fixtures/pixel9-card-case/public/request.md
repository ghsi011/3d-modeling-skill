# pixel9-card-case — the request

A downloaded, printed and in-service phone case with a card drawer, exported
from Bambu Studio as a production-extension 3MF: the root part carries the scene
and every mesh lives in its own part behind a `p:path`.

Two things are asked of it.

**Diagnose it.** Say what is in the archive, in what unit, at what placed size,
and whether anything can be built on it — without writing to the file, which is
the only authoritative copy.

**Modify it.** The drawer is the part a modification would touch; the case body
and the two card-retention parts are to survive unchanged.
